## Design Context

### Users
**Primary**: Founder demoing HotHotLeads live to **investors** during pitches and check-ins. Sessions are 2–10 minutes; investors are evaluating whether the product is *real*, *sophisticated*, and *valuable*.

**Secondary**: the same founder using the app daily between pitches to review today's hot leads, check signals, and trigger pipelines.

Used on desktop during live demos and on mobile for quick checks between meetings. Both must feel first-class — no second-class citizens.

### Brand Personality
**Fast · Dense · Modern.** Operator-grade, closer to a trading terminal or Linear than to consumer SaaS marketing. Confident, restrained, information-rich. Every screen earns its pixels.

Tone of writing: terse, declarative, specific. "12 leads today, avg score 74" beats "Today you've got some great new leads!"

### Aesthetic Direction
**Baseline**: shadcn/ui (Radix primitives + Tailwind) as the component foundation — customized in typography and color so it does *not* look like a stock shadcn template.

**Theme**: Both light and dark are first-class. Default to system preference with an accessible toggle.

**Color**: Neutral surfaces tinted warm (the "hot" brand association), with one confident accent — ember / vermilion — used sparingly for hot-state signals. Score semantics (hot / warm / cold) have dedicated tokens. No AI-slop purple/cyan gradients, no glow.

**Typography**: Explore beyond default Inter/Geist. Pair a dense, technical sans for UI with a characterful monospace for data, metrics, and the pipeline visualization. (Fonts like Haffer SQ, Manrope, Söhne-alternatives for sans; Commit Mono, JetBrains Mono, Berkeley-alternatives for mono — chosen at craft time.)

**Anti-references**: generic SaaS dashboards with centered hero metrics and identical card grids; glowing cyan-on-dark "AI" aesthetic; purple-to-blue gradients; big emoji above headings; glassmorphism; border-left accent stripes; gradient text.

### Design Principles
1. **Density is a feature, not a bug.** Investors want to see activity, not whitespace. Pack information purposefully; use tight rows and typographic rhythm, not padding, for hierarchy.
2. **Show the work.** Credibility comes from watching agents actually run. The live multi-agent pipeline visualization is the signature interaction.
3. **Operator over consumer.** Keyboard-first, tabular where appropriate, command-palette over modals. Respect the reader's time.
4. **Restraint over ornament.** One confident accent. Neutral surfaces. No decorative gradients, stripes, or glows. Visual interest comes from typography, rhythm, and motion at key moments.
5. **Both themes, both devices, equally good.** Mobile and light mode are not afterthoughts. Every component works at 360px and under direct sunlight.
